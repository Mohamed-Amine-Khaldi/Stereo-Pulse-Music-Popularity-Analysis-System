About This Project

How Stereo Pulse works

A 19-record music popularity catalog, built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the Web Programming final project.

Custom business rules

01

Top Charts eligibility

A track needs a popularity score of 50 or higher to be marked Top Charts. Anything below stays in the catalog but is labelled Not Charting and excluded from the charts bar graph.

02

Low Reach flag

Any track under 50 million streams is flagged Low Reach, regardless of its popularity score — a track can score well per listen but still have a small audience.

03

Genre cap

A track cannot carry more than 3 genres. The Data Management form blocks a 4th checkbox the moment it's ticked, with an inline message explaining why.

+

Trending badge (bonus)

Tracks growing 15% or more month-over-month get a 🔥 Trending badge on the Home and Data Management pages.
